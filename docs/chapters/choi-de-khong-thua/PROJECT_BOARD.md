## The direction

An eight-bamboo tile offered across a jade table. The visual turning point is an open hand and a changed understanding of the other person. Ivory paper and warm brass keep the scene intimate; coral distinguishes the offered tile and the change in perspective. The six editable SVGs are original geometric composition studies, not film stills or final scene illustrations. The author’s supplied manuscript is the source of the story, not the reference boards.

The board follows the shared `.json` grammar: a literary cover, illustrated A5 opener, desktop/mobile reader, five social cards, a vertical storyboard, scene studies, an optional interactive artifact and a production plan. It uses the composition of the Expected Utility and Dating Algorithm boards, with a distinct tabletop setting for this chapter.

## Interactive artifact — “Đổi góc nhìn”

The proposed explorer is a qualitative 2×2 reading of the essay. Rows: Rachel stays / leaves. Columns: Eleanor accepts / prevents the relationship. A two-state control compares Eleanor’s earlier assumptions with what the conversation gives her reason to reconsider. Selecting a cell reveals a question or implication grounded in the essay. Nick remains visible as an affected person outside this deliberately simplified two-player diagram.

There are no invented numeric utilities, probabilities, score sliders or automatically awarded “Nash equilibrium.” The model’s limits are visible: the essay itself says a rigorous equilibrium claim requires complete strategies and payoffs. The interaction adds comparison and context after the story; it is optional and does not replace the full chapter.

Acceptance for production: keyboard-operated controls; readable static default without JavaScript; all four combinations inspectable; clear before/after labels; touch layout; contrast and reduced-motion checks; reset to the initial view; original-source links for interpretations.
