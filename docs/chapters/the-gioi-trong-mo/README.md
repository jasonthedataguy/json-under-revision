## Production concept — The Borrowed Reel
Author-approved Paris rooftop at dusk with a small symbolic rat, Métro Line 5 to Bastille, two French-looking children (illustration choice: one blond, one brunette), an old keypad phone and an imagined family dinner. Palette: navy #172B3A, amber #DAB073, paper #F4EADB.

**Critical distinction:**
- **Observed:** narrator arrives in Paris with two suitcases, sees two children and skateboard on Métro Line 5, partly understands the call home.
- **Imagined:** home kitchen, family conversation, salmon, asparagus, schoolmate and meal. Never claim these things happened.
- **Afterimage:** persistent yellowish, low-contrast, grainy look found later in narrator's own edited photos.
- **Symbol:** rooftop rat overlooking Paris is **not in the prose** and is not intended to depict a copyrighted named movie character.
