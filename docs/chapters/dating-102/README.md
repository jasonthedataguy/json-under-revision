## Chapter idea
After **Dating Algorithm** and **Three Meeting Theory**, Jason tries to convince the audience that status-seeking dates are a zero-sum game and will eventually cause humanity's extinction. But the iPad drawing, weekly football, and humble Sunday picnic reveal an emotional desire he struggles to say directly: *someone who is curious about how his mind works.*

**Anchor quote:** “talking, dates, gifts are all rituals of love but being curious mới là actual intimacy.”

**Narrative role:** Overconfident 102 lecture → ordinary intimate details → borrowed Boston story → mistaken currency → demographic overreach → joke about patriotic dating.
