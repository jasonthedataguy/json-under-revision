## Narrative visual beats
1. **The Drop:** fashionable sneakers as a winter reward.
2. **The Cycle:** a new season, another reason to buy.
3. **The Rulebook:** item-matching strictures borrowed from others.
4. **The Remix:** copy, adopt, remove; personal style emerges.
5. **Patina:** marks, stains and wear become personal history.
6. **Hidden Gem:** a garment stored for two decades in a father's closet.
