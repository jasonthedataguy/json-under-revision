# Creative brief — Cơm nguội

## Creative thesis
An ordinary family gesture persists so long that a child forgets it began as an act of protection. Do **not** announce a moral; let the final line carry the feeling.

## Emotion
Quiet domestic warmth, restraint, care without grand gestures; a tiny visual distance between a hot pot and a bowl of leftover rice.

## From the strategy deck
Story / memory / version: READ / EXPLORE / REVISIT. The chapter page adds atmosphere, not necessary exposition. Proposed original illustration grammar: midnight, remembered warmth, paper, imperfect marks and restrained archival marks.

## Visible objects: permitted / invented
Text supports a bowl of cold rice, fresh hot rice, a family meal, a father, a child and their exchange. The window, city buildings, family portraits, lamp and clothing in the provisional illustration are artistic conjectures—**not documented details**. Need author approval before final art.

## Book
A5, readable Vietnamese serif, wide margins, one strong image, no dramatic pull quotes until after the reader reaches them, optional small archive panel after the last text. Four-page **layout proof only**. Final numbering pending complete manuscript architecture.

## Digital
Single URL on an owned domain; mobile-first HTML with illustration, timeline of three explicitly textual statements, reversible original/edit view, optional sound study. Do not automatically play audio, do not claim a real recording, do not capture user messages or analytics without a policy.

## Video
Five beats, 24 seconds in proof, room-tone bed; no fictional dialogue. Social discovery version can be 40–70 seconds with author voice only if recorded.

## QA gates
Author approves manuscript; art fidelity and privacy; final production illustration; printer proof; accessible mobile page; stable owned URL; tested QR; completed voice/subtitles; release signoff.
