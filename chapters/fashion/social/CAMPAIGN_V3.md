## Production directions for new exports
- Make first social frame a clear hook, not a mid-sentence excerpt.
- Crop per medium rather than putting a 16:9 source image inside thick letterbox bars. Preserve focal point at 9:16 1080x1920 and 4:5 1080x1350.
- 1–2 lines maximum on cover at phone scale; alt text and full words in caption.
- Keep header identity but give post creatives their own typographic hierarchy.
- Instagram carousel vs Instagram story should not be identical layouts.
- Review commercial rights of illustrated marks and music before any sponsored campaign.

## A/B validation (real data only)
- A: hook is identity ("WHO DID YOU DRESS FOR?")
- B: hook is memory ("THE THINGS WE KEPT")
- Primary: visits from trackable platform-specific links **without personal identifiers**.
- Secondary: opted-in readers, chapter-read completions and real print interest.
- No claims of conversion results until actual measurement exists.
